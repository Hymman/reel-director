# Reel Director — 90-second Demo Script

Use a fictional brand and original assets. Record the deployed English interface at 1080p. Keep the final public YouTube or Vimeo video under three minutes.

## 0:00–0:08 — Positioning

Show the Campaign Library and workspace navigation.

Narration: “Most AI video tools begin after you already know what to make. Reel Director starts by researching what is worth making and finishes a complete branded social campaign.”

## 0:08–0:22 — Controlled intake

Open New Campaign. Enter a concise fictional product brief and show URL/file context.

Narration: “Give it a brief, URL, image, video, PDF, or DOCX. It asks at most one clarification, always lets you skip, and turns the result into a reviewable Content Blueprint.”

## 0:22–0:36 — Live research and direction

Show source evidence and three creative directions.

Narration: “Parallel Search gathers current evidence. Gemini synthesizes audience tensions and creates three traceable creative directions grounded in those sources.”

## 0:36–0:56 — Director Studio and cost control

Show three candidate previews, select one, and choose Hybrid Reel.

Narration: “Director Studio shows three candidates before expensive video generation. The default Hybrid path sends only my selected shot to Veo, then adds controlled product frames, motion copy, captions, and CTA.”

## 0:56–1:10 — Campaign Pack

Show Hybrid Reel, Static Post, Carousel cards, Slideshow Reel, and cover.

Narration: “The same approved intelligence creates a complete campaign pack. Static and carousel outputs add no Veo cost, while the slideshow animates the cards into a vertical Reel.”

## 1:10–1:23 — Brand system and delivery

Show Brand Kit, playback, seeking, and separate downloads.

Narration: “A reusable Brand Kit controls the real logo, product visuals, colors, typography, caption style, and CTA. Playback supports seeking, and every asset downloads separately.”

## 1:23–1:30 — Close

Return to the Campaign Library.

Narration: “Reel Director: research it, direct it, ship the campaign.”
